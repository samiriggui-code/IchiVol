# What Are Cryptocurrency Trading Strategies?

Beginner

Updated Aug 21, 2026

## Key Takeaways

- A trading strategy is a structured plan that defines what you trade, how you manage positions, and how you apply [technical analysis (TA)](https://www.binance.com/en/academy/articles/what-is-technical-analysis) or [fundamental analysis (FA)](https://www.binance.com/en/academy/articles/what-is-fundamental-analysis-fa) to your decisions.

- Active strategies such as day trading and scalping require regular monitoring and are generally better suited to more experienced traders.

- Passive approaches such as buying and holding may be easier for beginners to manage consistently since they require less market monitoring.

- No single strategy suits everyone; the right approach depends on your goals, risk tolerance, available time, and experience level.

## Introduction

A trading strategy is a structured plan for all your trading activities. It acts as a framework to guide your decisions across different market conditions. Having a strategy doesn't guarantee profits, but it can help you stay consistent, [manage risk](https://www.binance.com/en/academy/articles/a-beginners-guide-to-understanding-risk-management), and avoid making impulsive choices during volatile periods.

This article covers some of the most commonly used cryptocurrency trading strategies. These range from short-term active methods to long-term passive approaches. They can also be applied to other asset classes, such as [stocks](https://www.binance.com/en/academy/articles/stock-trading-for-beginners-your-first-steps-to-take) or [foreign exchange.](https://www.binance.com/en/academy/articles/what-is-forex-trading)

## What Is a Trading Strategy?

A trading strategy is a structured plan that guides your buying and selling decisions. It covers which assets you trade, what signals trigger your entries and exits, where you place your [stop-loss orders](https://www.binance.com/en/academy/articles/what-are-stop-loss-and-take-profit-levels-and-how-to-calculate-them), how you [size your position](https://www.binance.com/en/academy/articles/how-to-calculate-position-size-in-trading) s, and how you track your performance over time. A clear strategy helps reduce [emotional decision-making](https://www.binance.com/en/academy/articles/trading-psychology-how-to-trade-without-emotions), especially during [volatile](https://www.binance.com/en/academy/glossary/volatility) markets.

Many traders verify their strategy before using real money by [backtesting](https://www.binance.com/en/academy/articles/what-is-backtesting) it against historical data or running it on a paper trading account. This can reveal weaknesses before any real capital is at risk. In practice, no two strategies are identical; many traders combine elements from multiple approaches or adjust their methods as they learn more about how different markets behave.

## Active Trading Strategies

Active strategies require significant time and attention. Good risk management is essential in all of them.

### Day trading

[Day trading](https://www.binance.com/en/academy/articles/a-beginners-guide-to-day-trading-cryptocurrency) involves entering and exiting positions within a single day, or in crypto’s case, within a 24-hour window. Day traders typically use price action and [technical indicators](https://www.binance.com/en/academy/articles/5-essential-indicators-used-in-technical-analysis) to find short-term opportunities. Because most cryptocurrency markets operate around the clock, the strategy has been adapted from its traditional finance ( [TradFi](https://www.binance.com/en/academy/glossary/tradfi)) origins.

Day trading can be demanding. It often requires fast decisions, close attention to price movements, and a strong grasp of order execution. For these reasons, it’s generally more suitable for experienced traders.

### Swing trading

[Swing trading](https://www.binance.com/en/academy/articles/a-beginners-guide-to-swing-trading-cryptocurrency) involves holding positions for more than a day but typically no longer than a few weeks. Swing traders aim to capture price movements that unfold over several days, often combining technical and fundamental factors when identifying trade setups.

Because trades play out over a longer horizon, swing traders do not need to watch the markets minute by minute. Many consider it a reasonable middle ground for those who want to trade actively without the intensity of intraday trading.

### Trend trading

Also called position trading, trend trading means holding positions for weeks or months to take advantage of sustained directional moves. Trend traders often rely on fundamental analysis to identify assets with long-term momentum, while using technical indicators such as [moving averages](https://www.binance.com/en/academy/articles/trading-strategies-with-moving-averages) and trend lines to time their entries and manage the possibility of a reversal.

This strategy requires patience, but it also tends to involve fewer individual trades, which can simplify decision-making.

### Scalping

[Scalping](https://www.binance.com/en/academy/articles/what-is-scalping-trading-in-cryptocurrency) focuses on profiting from very small price movements, repeated many times. Scalpers often hold positions for seconds or minutes. The strategy is closely linked to high-frequency trading and works best in liquid markets where entering and exiting positions is straightforward.

Scalping is considered an advanced strategy. Since scalping in cryptocurrency involves the smallest per-trade gains, larger position sizes are often needed to make the approach worthwhile.

## Passive Investment Strategies

Passive strategies focus on building a position over time rather than trading frequently. They generally require less screen time and less technical skill, which is part of why they are often described as more beginner-friendly.

### Buy and hold

Buy and hold means purchasing an asset and holding it over a long time horizon, regardless of short-term price swings. In crypto, this is sometimes called [HODL](https://www.binance.com/en/academy/articles/what-does-hodl-mean), a term that originated in the [Bitcoin](https://www.binance.com/en/academy/articles/what-is-bitcoin) community.

The idea is to look past temporary price swings and focus on long-term conviction in a project or asset. This approach avoids the stress of active trading, though it still carries risk if the asset declines in value over time.

### Dollar-cost averaging

[Dollar-cost averaging (DCA)](https://www.binance.com/en/academy/articles/dollar-cost-averaging-dca-explained) involves buying a fixed amount of an asset at regular intervals, regardless of price. By spreading purchases over time, DCA can reduce the impact of short-term volatility and remove the pressure of trying to time the market. It is frequently described as a straightforward, disciplined approach for newcomers.

### Index investing

Index investing spreads exposure across a basket of assets rather than concentrating on a single coin. A [crypto index fund](https://www.binance.com/en/academy/articles/what-is-a-crypto-index-fund), for example, may track several major digital assets, offering [diversified](https://www.binance.com/en/academy/articles/asset-allocation-and-diversification-explained) exposure in one position. This can help reduce the impact on your portfolio should one token collapse, though it does not remove market risk entirely.

## How to Choose a Strategy

There is no universally best strategy, and what works well for one person may not suit another. When deciding, it can help to weigh a few key factors:

- **Time**: Active strategies like day trading and scalping require far more attention than passive approaches.

- **Risk tolerance**: More frequent trading can mean higher potential gains but also greater potential losses.

- **Experience**: Passive strategies tend to have a gentler learning curve for beginners.

- **Goals**: Short-term trading and long-term accumulation call for different mindsets and tools.


Many beginners choose to allocate most of their capital to passive strategies while dedicating only a small portion to active trading as they learn. Whatever you decide, risk management remains essential.

## FAQ

### What is a cryptocurrency trading strategy?

A cryptocurrency trading strategy is a structured plan that guides your buying and selling decisions. It covers what assets you trade, what signals you use to enter or exit a position, how you manage position size, and how you protect yourself from large losses. A clear strategy helps reduce emotional decision-making during volatile markets.

### Which trading strategy is best for beginners?

Swing trading and buy and hold are often suggested as starting points. Swing trading gives you more time between decisions compared to day trading, while buy and hold requires the least active management. Beginners should pick a strategy that fits their available time, risk comfort level, and understanding of the market before committing real funds.

### What is the difference between active and passive trading strategies?

Active strategies, like day trading and scalping, involve frequent trades to capture short-term price moves and require close market monitoring. Passive strategies focus on accumulating and holding assets over the long term with less day-to-day involvement.

### How do I test a trading strategy before using real money?

You can backtest a strategy by running it against historical price data to see how it would have performed. Some exchanges offer paper trading or [testnet](https://www.binance.com/en/academy/glossary/testnet) environments where you can practice without risking real funds. Both approaches can help you identify weaknesses in your plan before you commit real capital.

### Do I need to stick to one strategy?

No. Many traders allocate different portions of their portfolio to different strategies and track each separately. This can help you understand what’s working and what isn’t. Strategies often evolve over time as you accumulate more trading experience and market knowledge.

## Closing Thoughts

Choosing a trading strategy that suits your schedule, goals, and experience level takes time. Keeping a [trading journal](https://www.binance.com/en/academy/articles/what-is-a-trading-journal-and-how-to-use-one) to track your decisions and outcomes can help you refine your methods over time.

You can also make use of tools like [copy trading](https://www.binance.com/en/academy/articles/copy-trading-a-game-changer-in-cryptocurrency-trading) or [trading bots](https://www.binance.com/en/academy/articles/what-are-crypto-trading-bots-and-how-do-they-work) to help you learn as you begin your trading journey, or experiment with these tools once you gain more confidence in your trading strategies.
